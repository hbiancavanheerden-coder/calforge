⚡ About CalForge

Your free, no-hassle calculator toolkit

What is CalForge?

CalForge is a free collection of 55+ online calculators and tools built for everyday people. Whether you're working out your mortgage repayments, converting units, tracking calories, splitting a restaurant bill, or planning for retirement — CalForge has a tool for it.

We built CalForge because too many calculator sites are cluttered, slow, require sign-ups, or are buried under ads before you even see the tool. CalForge is different: open the page, use the calculator, done.

Our Principles

Free, always. Every calculator on CalForge is completely free to use. No premium plans, no paywalls, no hidden costs.

No sign-up required. You don't need an account to use any tool on this site. Ever. Just open and calculate.

Privacy first. All calculations happen in your browser. We don't store your inputs or send them to any server. What you calculate stays with you.

Mobile friendly. CalForge is designed to work on phones, tablets, and desktops equally well.

Fast and simple. We keep interfaces clean, labels clear, and results instant.

What We Cover

Our calculators span six categories:

  • Finance — mortgage, loans, ROI, compound interest, savings, retirement, VAT, profit, currency, and more.
  • Health — BMI, BMR, calories, water intake, body fat, ideal weight, macros, and age.
  • Time & Productivity — time calculators, countdowns, study timers, Pomodoro, decision wheel, and more.
  • Converters — temperature, weight, area, length, cooking, file size, and currency.
  • Performance & Fitness — speed and pace calculators for runners and cyclists.
  • Home & Utility — paint, tiles, solar savings, electricity cost, and internet speed.

Accuracy & Formulas

We use standard, widely-accepted formulas for all calculators — the same ones used by financial institutions, health organisations, and research bodies. Results are estimates and should be validated with a qualified professional for important decisions. See our Disclaimer for more.
